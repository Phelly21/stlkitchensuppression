Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026.

Who We Are

STL Kitchen Suppression (stlkitchensuppression.com) is an independent service-request and referral resource for restaurant and commercial kitchen fire suppression service in Greater St. Louis, Missouri, and the Metro East area of Illinois. We are not a fire protection contractor.

Information We Collect

When you submit a request, we collect what you enter: your name, business name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, system brand, the service you need, urgency, and any details you add. If you call, we may receive your phone number and call details from our phone service, and calls may be recorded for quality and to handle your request. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may collect technical information such as your browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location.

How We Use It

  • To review and route your service request.
  • To share your request with an independent fire protection provider, only when you have given consent by checking the consent box or agreeing by phone.
  • To follow up with you about your request.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

Who We Share It With

With your consent, we share your request with one or more independent fire protection providers who serve your area so they can contact you about it. Those providers handle your information under their own policies. We also use service providers that help us run the site, process forms, and handle calls. We do not sell your information to list brokers or data resellers. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your Choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, or to delete the request information we hold, by emailing info@stlkitchensuppression.com. If a provider has already received your request, contact that provider directly about its records.

Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we receive, and we keep request records only as long as needed to handle requests and for ordinary business records.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.

Contact

Questions: info@stlkitchensuppression.com or (314) 314-8541.
